How Japan Became the Anime Capital of the World | The Story of Anime | Papel Tales (82 chars)

DESCRIPTION
Right now, a kid in Brazil is drawing giant sparkly eyes, someone in Paris is dressed as a ninja on the subway, and a family in Manila is arguing over which anime to watch. How did Japan become the anime capital of the world? This paper-cut animated story follows anime from the first hand-drawn cartoons of 1917 to Astro Boy, Akira, the 90s card craze, the Oscars and today's global streaming boom.
